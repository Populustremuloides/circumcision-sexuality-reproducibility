## Model uncertainty

For the direct calibrated posterior, the minimum positive adult share has median 2.19% and a 95% posterior interval of 0.81–3.46%. This interval propagates uncertainty in the current-status association, conditional on the observed covariate distribution and the endpoint assumptions. It does not incorporate an external timing distribution or validate historical counts. Required component ORs in the tables are illustrative point calculations; no confidence or credible intervals for those ORs are claimed.

## Reading the comparisons

The component baseline is the predicted never-circumcised prevalence standardized to the same covariate distribution as the constructed infant or adult component. The prevalence ratio therefore compares like distributions. It is distinct from the conditional odds ratio. Component shares may vary with covariates, while their survey-weighted mean equals the stated share.

The infant-only scenario fixes both childhood and adult groups at baseline. Setting only the adult group to baseline instead supports a preadult interpretation. Adult surgery having no effect on subsequent behavior does not establish that men selected for adult surgery had baseline-equivalent behavior beforehand. See [methods](methods.md).
