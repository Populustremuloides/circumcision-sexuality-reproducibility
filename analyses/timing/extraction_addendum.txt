Two mixture parameterizations: (a) constant timing fraction at each covariate pattern, flag any invalid cell and report weighted invalid fraction; (b) constant timing-component OR with covariate-varying fraction f(X)=[pC(X)-pU(X)]/[p_component(X)-pU(X)]. Solve component OR to attain the desired weighted timing budget. Parameterization(b) is a constructed explanation, not fitted timing data. Its minimum positive budget is weighted mean[(pC-pU)/(1-pU)], attained only at component outcome prevalence1. It preserves covariate-level mixtures exactly. Use it for readable required-OR tables.
